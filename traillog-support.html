Record with E500 Heart Rate

Traillog can use an E500 H Band watch for live heart rate while your iPhone records the activity, timing, distance and route. Other H Band models depend on device support.

  1. Wear the watch off its charger. Force-quit H Band on your iPhone to release its Bluetooth connection.
  2. In Traillog, open Settings > Health & Fitness > Heart Rate Sensors, tap Start Scanning, and connect to the watch.
  3. Wait for a real BPM value, then leave the sensor screen and start your activity in Traillog. The remembered watch reconnects for the activity.
  4. Pause, resume and stop in Traillog. Save once, then check the heart-rate chart in the saved activity. Ending the activity releases the watch so H Band can reconnect.

If BPM stops updating, check the sensor warning. Activity recording continues while the app tries to reconnect. Keep the watch nearby, check Bluetooth permission in iPhone Settings, and reconnect from Heart Rate Sensors if recovery stops. H Band readings stay on your iPhone and are not sent by Traillog to Apple Health or iCloud.

Saved Watch Data and H Band App History

Settings > Health & Fitness > H Band Watches reads supported records saved by the watch. Available workouts and measurements vary by model. It does not import phone-only H Band activities labeled App exercise or workouts shared as PNG images. For live recording with E500, use Heart Rate Sensors and start the activity in Traillog.

Apple Watch with an iPhone-Started Activity

Install Traillog on your Apple Watch, wear and unlock it, allow the requested Health access, and keep it near your iPhone. Start the activity in Traillog on iPhone. When the Watch workout starts successfully, it joins the same timeline activity with heart rate and controls on both devices.

If the Watch cannot start, the iPhone activity keeps recording. Check Bluetooth, Health permissions and the Watch app. A failed Watch start should not end the activity on iPhone. When reporting an issue, include both app versions and whether heart rate, pause/resume, stopping, or the saved timeline entry was affected.
